# Launch video (49.5 s)

- `brag.mp4`: the video (1920×1080, 30 fps, with music). `brag.jpg`: poster frame (also frame 0 of the video).
- `brag-plan.md`: angle and storyboard. `share-copy.txt`: text to post with it.

**What it shows:** the real SeaGrid page, captured frame by frame.
1. The SIMULATED demo off Constanța: MV SEA FALCON goes dark near a subsea cable, a loitering boat, a hydrophone
   and the cable fibre add evidence, the score reaches 100/100 and a patrol unit is sent.
2. The click on **Live**: REAL AIS recorded around Varna (aisstream.io, 12 October 2026, 10:45 UTC).
3. A REAL Sentinel-1D radar pass over Varna (04:13 UTC that day, 40 ship-sized targets).
4. **Acknowledge & engage** and **Copy tasking** on a live alert. Patrol units are always simulated.

Every number on screen comes from the pipeline's own timelines, not from the video. The radar pass came before the
AIS recording started, so its targets show as "not checked": the video says the radar found 40 ships, not that it
caught a dark vessel. The music and sound effects are synthesized.

Not kept here: the render sources and the 1,485 rendered frames (regenerable with `/brag`).
